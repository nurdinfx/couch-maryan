Everything You Need for Higher Education

Explore the deep capabilities of Guray Sicid Learning Platform designed to elevate student achievement, simplify faculty administrative overhead, and deliver interactive digital learning.

📹

High-Definition Video Lecture Streaming

Integrated custom video player with HTTP byte-range streaming, custom speed throttles (0.75x, 1x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 2x), wide screen mode, keyboard shortcut controls, and real-time millisecond watch progress tracking.

  • ✓ Buffer-less byte streaming for MP4, MOV, and WebM
  • ✓ 80% completion requirement calculation
  • ✓ Automatic resume from last watched position
📝

Interactive Examination Runner

Full-featured exam engine supporting single choice, multiple choice, True/False cards, short answers, and essay responses with dynamic countdown timers and immediate automated grading.

  • ✓ Instant automatic grading for objective questions
  • ✓ Real-time countdown timer with auto-submit
  • ✓ Question attempt history with detailed answer explanations
📊

Comprehensive Gradebook Matrix

Automated student performance matrix that aggregates weighted percentages across assignments, midterm exams, quizzes, and laboratory work into real-time letter grades and GPAs.

  • ✓ Configurable category weighting rubrics
  • ✓ Automatic GPA calculation and transcript views
  • ✓ Exportable grade records for faculty administration
📂

Assignments & Rubric Evaluations

Online assignment workspace with downloadable faculty specification attachments, student file uploads, deadline tracking, professor commentary, and resubmission requests.

  • ✓ PDF, ZIP, code archive submission handling
  • ✓ Timely submission timestamps and late penalties
  • ✓ Confidential professor feedback channel
📅

Attendance Tracking & Early Risk Alerts

Digital attendance records for every lecture and laboratory session. Flags students with unexcused absences or low attendance percentages to trigger proactive academic support.

  • ✓ Present, Late, Excused, and Absent statuses
  • ✓ Automated "At Risk" flags for low attendance (<80%)
  • ✓ Session notes and approved medical excuse records
🏅

Digital Badges & Verified Certificates

Gamified learning milestones and officially verifiable completion certificates generated with tamper-proof UUIDs that employers and institutions can verify online.

  • ✓ Automatic award criteria (Quiz Master, Perfect Attendance)
  • ✓ Public certificate verification by unique UUID
  • ✓ Downloadable PDF certificates with student credentials
💬

Direct Messaging & Course Forums

Built-in multimedia direct messenger allowing students and professors to communicate privately with attachment uploads, alongside course discussion boards for peer learning.

  • ✓ 1-on-1 private messaging with unread badges
  • ✓ Image and document attachments in messages
  • ✓ Pinned discussion topics and threaded replies
🌐

Full Bilingual English & Somali Experience

Complete bilingual support across every page, dialog, and interface element. Toggle between Somali and English instantly with zero page reloads.

  • ✓ One-click language switch anytime
  • ✓ Native Somali academic terminology
  • ✓ Preserves language preference in user profile
🎓

5-Year Academic Level Student Scoping

Structured course catalog with enrollment scoping from Year 1 through Year 5. Ensures students view and access the exact curriculum assigned to their academic standing.

  • ✓ Granular year-level course visibility rules
  • ✓ Admin student account academic year promotion
  • ✓ Cross-year elective and open access course support
