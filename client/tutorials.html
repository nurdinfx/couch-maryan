Bilingual Platform Video Tutorials

Master the Guray Sicid Learning Platform with comprehensive video guides available in English and Somali. Choose your preferred tutorial language below.

English • 3:45

1. Introduction to the Platform

A guided overview of the platform layout, navigation drawer, switching between dark and light modes, and bilingual language toggling.

  1. Navigate using the left drawer menu (Dashboard, Courses, Grades).
  2. Toggle between English and Somali via the top bar button.
  3. Access site-wide search for courses and assignments.
💡 Tip: You can collapse the navigation drawer anytime by clicking the hamburger icon (☰) at the top left.
English • 2:30

2. How to Sign In to Your Account

Learn how students, professors, and administrators authenticate securely using their university email credentials.

  1. Click the "Student Login" button on the homepage.
  2. Enter your registered university email and password.
  3. Use the password visibility icon (eye) to verify accuracy.
💡 Tip: If you forget your credentials, contact the platform administrator to issue a temporary reset key.
English • 4:15

3. How to Access Enrolled Courses

Explore how to find your assigned curriculum, view course modules, announcements, and track your overall percentage progress.

  1. Go to "Course Catalog" or your "Dashboard".
  2. Select your active enrolled course (e.g. CS301).
  3. Browse weekly modules and required learning tasks.
💡 Tip: Students only see courses matching their academic year level (Year 1 to Year 5).
English • 3:10

4. How to Watch Video Lessons

Master the built-in video player, keyboard shortcuts, speed controls, cinema mode, and automatic completion tracking.

  1. Open any video activity inside a course module.
  2. Use speed buttons (1x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 2x) to adjust pace.
  3. Watch at least 80% to register an automatic completion mark.
💡 Tip: Press the 'F' key on your keyboard to quickly toggle full-screen theater viewing.
English • 3:20

5. Downloading Syllabi & Documents

Learn how to view lecture slides inline, download assignment PDF specifications, and submit files for grading.

  1. Click on any document activity marked with the PDF icon.
  2. Use the browser's built-in inline reader or click Download.
  3. Review the instructor's submission guidelines carefully.
💡 Tip: Upload your assignment solutions in PDF or ZIP format before the due date to avoid late penalties.
English • 4:00

6. Tracking Grades & Earning Badges

How to view your gradebook matrix, check attendance percentages, earn honors badges, and claim your completion certificate.

  1. Click "Gradebook Matrix" in the navigation drawer.
  2. Check professor feedback notes on completed assignments.
  3. Verify completed course certificates using the verification UUID.
💡 Tip: Scoring 90% or higher on a midterm exam automatically awards the Quiz Master badge!
Af-Soomaali • 3:45

1. Hordhaca Guud ee Nidaamka

Muuqaal faahfaahsan oo sharxaya qaab-dhismeedka nidaamka, menu-ga bidix, baddalaadda luuqadda Soomaaliga/Ingiriiska, iyo qaabka habeen/maalin.

  1. Adeegso menu-ga bidix (Dashboard-ka, Koorsooyinka, Dhibcaha).
  2. Ku baddal luuqadda Soomaali/Ingiriis badhanka kore ee calanka.
  3. U isticmaal sanduuqa baadigoobka si aad u hesho cashar kasta.
💡 Talo Muhiim ah: Waxaad menu-ga qarin kartaa adigoo gujinaya astaanta saddexda xariiqood (☰).
Af-Soomaali • 2:30

2. Sida loo Galo Akoonka (Login)

Baro sida ardayda iyo macallimiintu ugu galaan nidaamka si ammaan ah iyagoo adeegsanaya email-kooda rasmiga ah.

  1. Guji badhanka "Galitaanka Ardayda" ee ku yaal bogga hore.
  2. Geli email-kaaga jaamacadda iyo furahaaga sirta ah (password).
  3. Guji astaanta isha si aad u xaqiijiso password-ka saxnaantiisa.
💡 Talo Muhiim ah: Haddii aad ilowdo password-kaaga, la xiriir maamulaha nidaamka si laguu siiyo mid cusub.
Af-Soomaali • 4:15

3. Sida loo Helo Koorsooyinkaaga

Muuqaal ku tusaya sida aad u helayso koorsooyinka aad ka diiwaangashan tahay, modul-lada casharrada, iyo ogeysiisyada macallinka.

  1. Tag qaybta "Koorsooyinka" ama Dashboard-kaaga.
  2. Dooro koorsada aad rabto inaad barato (tusaale CS301).
  3. Daalaco modul-kasta iyo hawlaha waxbarasho ee ku dhex jira.
💡 Talo Muhiim ah: Arday kasta wuxuu arkaa kaliya koorsooyinka u qoondeysan fasalkiisa (Sanadka 1 ilaa Sanadka 5).
Af-Soomaali • 3:10

4. Daawashada Casharrada Muuqaalka

Baro sida loo isticmaalo video player-ka casriga ah, kordhinta xawaaraha, shaashadda weyn, iyo qorista horumarkaaga.

  1. Guji cashar kasta oo muuqaal ah (Video lecture).
  2. Adeegso xawaaraha (1x, 1.25x, 1.5x) si aad ugu xawaareysato.
  3. Daawo ugu yaraan 80% si casharku kuugu noqdo mid dhammaystiran.
💡 Talo Muhiim ah: Riix batoonka 'F' ee keyboard-ka si aad shaashadda oo dhan uga dhigto mid buuxda.
Af-Soomaali • 3:20

5. Soo Dejinta Manhajka & Dukumiintiyada

Sida loo aqriyo slides-ka macallinka, loo soo dejiyo manhajka PDF-ka ah, iyo sida loo gudbiyo jawaabaha layliyada.

  1. Guji fayl kasta oo leh astaanta PDF-ka ee casaan ah.
  2. Ku dhex fur browser-ka ama guji Download si aad ugu shubato qalabkaaga.
  3. U diyaarso shaqadaada qaab PDF ah inta aan waqtigu dhicin.
💡 Talo Muhiim ah: Gudbi layliyadaada waqtiga loogu talagalay si looga fogaado dhibco goyn.
Af-Soomaali • 4:00

6. La Socodka Dhibcaha & Shahaadada

Muuqaal ku tusaya sida aad u ogaan karto natiijadaada, xaadiriskaaga, billadaha aad heshay, iyo xaqiijinta shahaadada qallinjebinta.

  1. Tag qaybta "Dhibcaha & Natiijada" (Gradebook Matrix).
  2. Akhri faallooyinka uu macallinku kaga soo jawaabay shaqadaadii.
  3. Ka hel shahaado rasmi ah markaad koorsada si buuxda u gudubto.
💡 Talo Muhiim ah: Ardayda keenta 90% ama ka badan waxay si toos ah u helaan billadda "Quiz Master"!
